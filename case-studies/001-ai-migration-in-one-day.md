# AI Migration in One Day

This case study describes a rapid migration of an AI workflow using the orchestration framework.

## Situation

A team needed to consolidate legacy AI services and move to a unified orchestration platform within a single day.

## Approach

- Assess existing AI models, prompts, and integrations
- Define the target orchestration flow and governance requirements
- Use reusable context templates and agent coordination patterns

## Outcome

- Migrated core AI workflow with minimal downtime
- Improved observability and validation controls
- Enabled faster iteration on future enhancements
