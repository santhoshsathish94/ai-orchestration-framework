# CLOVER — 30-Second Gemini Video Prompts

## Purpose

Generate the first public-facing CLOVER explainer as one coherent ~30-second cinematic sequence using a starting image plus three continuation prompts.

Core message:

> **CLOVER is a way of working with AI to reach real outcomes.**

The story must stay human-simple while preserving the framework's deeper principles:

- AI can be highly capable and still fail.
- Incomplete context is a major source of failure.
- The real system already contains useful context.
- Humans retain purpose, ownership, judgment, boundaries, and approvals.
- AI performs substantial work within those boundaries.
- Reality—not AI confidence—validates Success.
- A previous run succeeding many times does not guarantee the next run.
- Failed validation should lead back to Context and new information.
- Growth is the fifth leaf, shown as learning and curiosity rather than prediction.
- Capability may scale, but Direction remains human.

---

# Global continuity rules

Apply these rules to the starting image and every continuation.

### Primary human

One consistent adult professional throughout the entire video.

Appearance:
- late 30s to early 40s
- natural, intelligent, calm expression
- simple modern professional clothing
- no visible company branding
- realistic human proportions

Do not change gender, age, clothing, hairstyle, face, or body proportions between continuations.

### Environment

One coherent modern engineering workspace that can visually reveal a larger connected system.

Start with a focused desk/workstation and a laptop or large monitor showing an AI conversation.

The environment later expands visually to reveal the surrounding system: application behavior, source code, logs, data, tests, system history, and running environment.

Do not teleport to unrelated locations.

### Visual style

- realistic cinematic live-action
- bright, light, clean environment
- restrained futuristic visual language
- subtle warm natural light
- believable materials and screens
- shallow depth of field where appropriate
- smooth camera movement
- premium documentary / technology film aesthetic
- no cyberpunk
- no dark sci-fi mood
- no robot characters
- no evil AI imagery
- no giant holographic interfaces
- no code rain
- no model logos

### Camera

Use one coherent visual language:
- slow cinematic dolly and pull-back movements
- deliberate framing
- no frantic cuts
- no camera whiplash
- visual continuity across prompts

### AI representation

The AI should be represented as a useful digital assistant/interface, not as a humanoid robot.
It should visibly reason and work across connected information sources.

### Text

Use minimal on-screen text.
Prefer very short labels:
- Context
- Direction
- Action
- Success
- CLOVER

The generated video should prioritize visuals and voiceover over complex UI text.

# Starting Image Prompt

Create the first frame of a realistic cinematic 30-second technology story about a human working with AI to solve a real engineering problem.

A single professional adult is seated at a clean modern workstation in a bright contemporary engineering office. The person is looking at a large monitor displaying a simple AI assistant conversation. The AI has just produced a polished, confident answer to a difficult system problem. The person looks attentive and slightly uncertain rather than impressed or frightened.

The screen should communicate a plausible engineering issue without requiring readable code. Keep the interface simple and believable: a short problem description, a confident AI response, and subtle visual hints that the answer may not match the actual system.

The workstation and surrounding environment should feel real and lived-in, not futuristic. In the deep background, subtly hint at a larger digital environment that can later be revealed: application telemetry, source repositories, data, tests, and running services represented naturally through monitors, server/environment indicators, and connected visual elements.

Composition: medium shot, subject slightly off-center, monitor visible, enough surrounding environment to allow a later cinematic pull-back.

Lighting: bright natural daylight with soft professional office illumination.

Style: premium realistic cinematic technology documentary, believable human emotion, restrained visual effects, clean light palette, high detail, consistent physical environment.

Do not show the CLOVER diagram yet. Do not show four or five leaves yet. Do not show a robot. Do not show a futuristic city. Do not make the AI look evil. Do not use dramatic warning imagery.

The first frame should feel like an ordinary real work problem that many people could recognize.

---

# Prompt 1 — Problem → Missing Context

Continue directly from the starting image. Preserve the exact same human, clothing, face, workstation, office, monitor design, lighting, color palette, camera language, and realistic cinematic style.

The person briefly follows the AI's confident recommendation. Then a subtle mismatch appears: the real system behavior on another part of the screen does not agree with the AI's answer. The person notices the discrepancy. Do not portray the AI as stupid; the answer should look reasonable and competent, but incomplete because the AI did not have the whole picture.

The camera slowly pulls back from the narrow AI conversation view into the larger environment. As the view widens, reveal that the surrounding system already contains the missing information: source code and repository history, live logs and telemetry, relevant data, test results, deployment/runtime state, and previous investigation notes. Represent these as believable screens and environmental signals, not giant holograms.

The key visual idea is: the information was already there; it was simply outside the AI's initial view.

End this continuation with the word **Context** appearing simply and clearly for a moment, while the human remains visible within the larger environment.

Voiceover timing for this section should feel natural and conversational:

"AI can be incredibly capable. But it can still get the answer wrong when it doesn't have the whole picture. The information was already there—in the system, the data, the logs, the history."

Do not introduce the full CLOVER loop yet. Do not show Direction, Action, Success, or the fifth leaf as labels yet.

Target duration for this continuation: approximately 10–12 seconds total including the initial problem beat and context reveal.

---

# Prompt 2 — Direction → Action

Continue the exact same scene and visual world from the final frame of Prompt 1. Preserve the exact same primary human, clothing, face, workstation, environment, lighting, physical props, and cinematic camera language.

The human now clearly takes ownership of the task. They indicate the relevant part of the connected system and communicate a simple outcome and boundary to the AI. Visually, make this understandable without technical jargon: one area is highlighted as the problem to solve, another area is clearly marked as something not to change, and a simple destination state is indicated.

The human remains present and visibly responsible. Do not show the person handing everything over or disappearing.

Introduce the label **Direction** briefly and elegantly.

Then the AI begins substantial work. The camera follows a smooth movement across the connected workspace as the AI reads the relevant context, traces the problem, reasons across multiple pieces of information, makes a focused change, and runs a test/check. Show meaningful activity, not generic typing. The AI is doing the execution, while the human remains the accountable owner.

Avoid implying that the AI simply follows a fixed script. Show adaptive reasoning and focused execution.

End with the work apparently complete and the system ready to provide an independent verdict.

Voiceover for this section:

"Then humans set the direction—what matters, what must not happen, and what success should look like. AI figures out how to do the work and acts within those boundaries."

Target duration: approximately 9–10 seconds.

Do not show the fifth leaf yet. Keep the story grounded and practical.

---

# Prompt 3 — Success → Ownership → CLOVER → Growth

Continue directly from the previous final frame. Preserve the exact same human and complete visual world. No character or environment redesign.

Now show that the AI's own statement of completion is not the final verdict. The real environment independently checks the intended outcome.

Show a simple, universally understandable validation transformation: the original failing signal changes to the desired state. For example, an error indicator disappears while a corresponding test/check becomes valid, or a system behavior visibly changes from failing to working. Make the cause-and-effect obvious without using dense dashboards.

Briefly show the AI interface as if it could say "Done," but immediately make it clear that the environment itself is what decides. The human watches the evidence.

Introduce the label **Success** with a short visual caption:

**Reality is the evidence.**

Then show the human still standing beside the process—not replaced, not absent, still clearly accountable.

The camera smoothly pulls back. The four stages appear as a clean, elegant loop:

**Context → Direction → Action → Success**

Keep the diagram visually simple and integrated into the environment, not as a corporate presentation slide.

Then subtly reveal a fifth leaf growing from the completed four-stage form. It should feel organic, intriguing, and calm. Do not depict danger, evil, takeover, humanoid robots, or apocalypse. The meaning is simply that each cycle leaves learning for the next.

Finish on a clean bright identity frame with:

**CLOVER**

and below it:

**Context → Direction → Action → Success**

The fifth leaf should remain visible but understated.

Voiceover:

"Then reality decides: did it actually work? That's CLOVER—a way of working with AI to reach real outcomes. Humans keep the ownership. And every cycle leaves experience for the next."

Target duration: approximately 10 seconds.

End calmly, with enough visual breathing room for the final CLOVER title to be understood.

# Optional alternative final voiceover

For a version aimed at a more technically mature audience, use:

"That's CLOVER: Context, Direction, Action, Success. Four leaves make the framework. The fifth is what repeated cycles can teach us."

Do not use wording that suggests the fifth leaf is AI acquiring ownership, autonomy, or a new destination.

# Negative / exclusion guidance for all generations

Do not generate:
- AI replacing the human
- autonomous humanoid robot behavior
- evil AI
- red warning screens
- apocalypse / dystopian city imagery
- giant glowing brain imagery
- code rain
- excessive holograms
- multiple unrelated characters
- model logos
- vendor branding
- dense unreadable dashboards
- generic business stock-video gestures
- constant rapid scene changes
- impossible teleportation between environments
- inconsistent clothing or face
- exaggerated science-fiction interfaces
- AI selecting the destination or organizational purpose
- imagery suggesting capability automatically grants authority

The story is about a practical way of working with capable AI in a real environment while keeping human Direction and accountability explicit.

# Production Notes

1. Generate the starting image first.
2. Generate Prompt 1 as a continuation from that exact starting image.
3. Generate Prompt 2 as a continuation from the final frame of Prompt 1.
4. Generate Prompt 3 as a continuation from the final frame of Prompt 2.
5. Review the first successful ~30-second result before attempting additional extensions.
6. Do not keep adding prompts merely to increase duration.
7. If continuity breaks, regenerate the affected continuation from the correct prior frame instead of adding another narrative branch.
8. Preserve the same primary human and visual environment across all generations.
9. Keep the first version practical. Save deeper speculative questions about increasing capability for a separate future-facing communication piece once the audience understands the four-stage cycle.
