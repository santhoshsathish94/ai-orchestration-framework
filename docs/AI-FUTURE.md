# The AI Future

The standalone technical hypothesis is maintained in [ai-future-hypothesis.md](ai-future-hypothesis.md).

It is intentionally **not part of the AI Orchestration Framework lifecycle**. It explores a possible future in which persistent experience, reusable expertise, AI-to-AI interaction, and governed shared learning could lead toward collective intelligence.

The hypothesis is exploratory: it is meant to be tested, challenged, and refined as the technology evolves.
